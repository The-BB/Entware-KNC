    realtek: dsa: trap EAPOL frames to the CPU on RTL93xx
    
    EAPOL frames must not be forwarded to other ports. Trap them to the CPU so
    that an 802.1X authenticator such as hostapd can handle them. RTL83xx
    already does this via SPCL_TRAP_EAPOL_CTRL.
