    realtek: phy: add delay to fix pcie link up failures
    
    The current pcie phy driver results in inconsistent failures on PCIe linkup,
    different from one boot to another. Doing some testing, it has became clear
    that mdio operations are at fault. The mdio seem to require an additional
    delay after it has completed an operation and reported status of done. The
    mdio ready bit however, is unchanged and so doesn't have any connection to
    this "after completion" delay.
    
    Fix the issue by adding an additional delay after regmap polling and remove
    MDIO_READY from cond variable as it is unrelated. Change the function name
    to rtk_phy_poll_done_and_delay() to go along with the changes. Mostly as a
    guess but 10 us is enough delay to fix the inconsistent failures. Tested by
    booting an image on rtl9607c machine 4 times and no failed link ups happen.
    
    Add the fsleep() after regmap_read() in rtk_phy_read() as it also triggers
    something in phy mdio controller that require the same delay.
