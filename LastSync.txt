    realtek: fix PCI IO map panic with set_io_port_base(KSEG1)
    
    When the realtek target changed to generic mips initialization
    the set_io_port_base(KSEG1); from plat_mem_setup wasn't brought
    over to the realtek board file. This results in boot hang when
    trying to load PCIE driver on RTL9607C machine as it panics
    after devm_pci_alloc_host_bridge due to lack of mips_io_port_base
    definition.
    
    Fix it by adding set_io_port_base(KSEG1) to realtek_fixup_fdt.
    Generic MIPS calls fixup_fdt during plat_mem_setup so that
    roughly matches that of an old initialization from before.
    
    As this changes the board file, refresh the 300-03 patch to
    align with the new changes.
