    realtek: eth: rtl93xx: say that the RMA setup traps
    
    rteth_930x_set_rx_mode() and rteth_931x_set_rx_mode() say they flood
    every class of RMA address while the conduit is promiscuous, like the
    RTL838x version. All three write 3 into every 2-bit action field of
    RMA_CTRL_0..2. In the GPL SDK that is flood on RTL838x
    (dal_maple_trap_rmaGroupAction_set()), but trap to the master CPU on
    RTL930x and RTL931x (dal_longan_trap_rmaAction_set(),
    dal_mango_trap_rmaAction_set()), which have no flood action for these
    addresses at all. On an RTL9303, frames to 01:80:C2:00:00:0B and
    01:80:C2:00:00:21 reach the CPU with the trapped-RMA reason, 28.
    
    Trapping is also what fits here: whether such a frame goes any further
    is for the bridge to decide. Keep the values and correct the comments.
    
    No functional change.
