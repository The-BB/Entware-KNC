    qualcommax: qca_ppe: add 10 and 100 Mbps half duplex
    
    The ports list only full duplex in their MAC capabilities, so the PHY
    advertises no half-duplex mode. A link partner with autonegotiation off
    is found by parallel detection, which resolves to half duplex, and the
    port does not link.
    
    Add MAC_10HD and MAC_100HD to ports 1-6. The GMAC programs half duplex;
    USXGMII runs on the XGMAC, which has no duplex setting, so
    mac_get_caps() removes both modes for that interface.
    
    On a Xiaomi AX3600 (QCA8075) with the partner forced to 10 or 100 Mbps,
    the port stays down before the change and links at half duplex after
    it, 3/3 runs each.
