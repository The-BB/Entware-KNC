    base-files: keep kernel and rootfs UBI volume IDs with provisioning
    
    Since sysupgrade creates the provisioning volume by default, it takes the
    lowest free volume ID before the kernel and rootfs volumes are created.
    This shifts their IDs by one, so devices that hardcode the rootfs volume
    in root= (e.g. root=/dev/ubiblock0_1) no longer boot after a sysupgrade.
    
    Create the provisioning volume with the last volume ID instead.
    
    A provisioning volume that already exists at a low ID is kept and has to
    be removed by hand.
    
    Fixes: 725a1dc534a1 ("base-files: create the provisioning partition on sysupgrade by default")
    Fixes: https://github.com/openwrt/openwrt/issues/25501
