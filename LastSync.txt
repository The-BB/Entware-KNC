    airoha: add the missing PHY_AIROHA_AN7583_PCIE config symbol
    
    The AN7583 PCIe PHY driver adds a Kconfig symbol that neither subtarget
    config carries, so syncconfig prompts for it and every airoha kernel
    build fails. Enable it on an7583 and leave it off on an7581.
