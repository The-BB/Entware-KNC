    realtek: rtl839x: trap slow protocols to the cpu
    
    The switch drops frames to 01-80-C2-00-00-02 (slow protocols). On
    RTL839x the RMA action is FORWARD (0) normally and FLOOD (3) with
    promisc/allmulti. Neither reaches the CPU. This breaks LACP bonding.
    
    This improved code always sets the action for 01-80-C2-00-00-02 to
    TRAP2CPU (2) and always sets the STP and VLAN bypass. So STP and VLAN
    filtering do not drop them.
    
    With promisc/allmulti, all RMA addresses are now trapped instead of
    flooded, as flooded frames do not reach the CPU either.
    
    The registers are now updated with regmap_update_bits(), so the
    reserved bits of RMA_CTRL_0 and the bits above 9 of RMA_CTRL_3 are no
    longer cleared. RMA_CTRL_3 is now also set in allmulti mode, so the
    promisc bypass bits do not stay set after a change to allmulti.
