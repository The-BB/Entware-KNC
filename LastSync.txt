    ca-certificates: blacklist "emSign ECC Root CA - G3"
    
    This blacklists the "emSign ECC Root CA - G3" CA certificate which
    issued some intermediate CA certificates for eMudhra with 8 bit private
    keys between 0 and 255.
    
    "Comodo AAA Services root" is also blacklisted by Debian, but already
    removed in update 20260223.
