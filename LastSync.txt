    qualcommax: ipq60xx: apply the 512 MB memory layout on 512 MB boards
    
    ipq6018-cp-cpu.dtsi includes ipq6018-mp5496.dtsi, which includes
    ipq6018.dtsi again and restores the 85 MB q6_region over the 55 MB one
    from ipq6018-512m.dtsi. The 360 V6, AP120C-AX, FAP650, MR7500, RBR350
    and RBS350 lose 30 MB of RAM this way. The MR7350 and WAX214 have
    512 MB and set qcom,ath11k-fw-memory-mode = <1>, but do not include the
    512 MB layout at all.
    
    Drop the SoC include from ipq6018-512m.dtsi and include the layout after
    ipq6018-cp-cpu.dtsi. The q6_region size is the only change in the
    compiled device trees of these boards; the trees of the other IPQ60xx
    boards do not change.
    
    Not run on these boards, which are not available here.
