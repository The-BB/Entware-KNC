    realtek: pie: rtl839x: place the log index at bit 6
    
    A PIE rule on RTL839x counts the packets it matches into the counter
    named by its LOG_IDX field, bits 80:70 of the entry (Realtek GPL SDK,
    rtk_cypress_tableField_list.c), which are bits 16:6 of r[14]. The
    writer puts the index at bits 14:4, so the hardware reads it divided
    by four, and bit 0 of the index lands in MIR_IDX.
    The reader shifts the same way, so the route dump in debugfs reads the
    counter the driver allocated rather than the one the hardware fills.
    
    Write and read the index at bit 6.
    
    Tested on an HPE 1920-48G (RTL8393) with two offloaded routes, which
    got packet counters 0 and 1, and 8 echo requests through one route at
    a time. Before, the requests through the second route added 8 to the
    first route's counter and nothing to its own. After, each route's
    counter grew by 8 for its own requests and stayed put for the other's.
    Counters above 1 were not exercised.
